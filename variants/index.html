Website prototype

Six site variants, one design.

Each variant is a complete site built around one angle: a home page, six service pages, an about page, and the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy. Every page uses the landing-page design as it is on Webflow today (staging.credolegal.com) and today's landing-page copy.

"Perfect fit" means the cluster has a landing page at exactly that angle. "Best fit" is the closest available page.
